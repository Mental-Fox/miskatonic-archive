# Растровые поля пергамента

Созданы встроенным ImageGen. Прозрачность исходных PNG сохранена. Файлы используются на основных разворотах и тайных листах; сами иллюстрации существ и пользовательские замены не изменены.

- scattered-rlyeh-v1.png — рукописные знаки на прозрачном фоне, 1024 × 1536.
- engraving-atlas-v1.png — восемь гравюрных деталей, 1774 × 887; сетка 4 × 2 без видимых разделителей.

Порядок деталей: паутина, щупальце, кровь, лёд; подпалины, печать, когти, перо с чернильной кляксой.

## Запрос для знаков

Create one production bitmap asset: a transparent overlay for an antique Lovecraft grimoire page, portrait 2:3 format. On completely transparent background, draw about 70 unique invented R'lyeh occult sigils as aged dark brown and faded black quill ink marks. They are chaotic marginalia, NOT rows, NOT a border, NOT a font chart, NOT vector clean line art. Scattered asymmetric clusters of tiny cryptic glyphs, scratched spirals, half erased crooked inscriptions, rubbed out notes, ink scratches, small droplets. Most marks distributed along the outer 20 percent of left and right margins and in scattered top and bottom corner groups; the central 60 percent remains mostly empty for readable book text. Vary sizes and rotations strongly, tiny groups of 2-4 characters at odd diagonals, some partly erased and faint, some a little darker. Genuine scanned antique ink texture, dry pen fibers and grain in every stroke, imperfect pressure, historical manuscript appearance, muted sepia. No paper rectangle, no background color, no readable Latin words, no uniform repeat grid, no geometric icon style, no UI. Actual transparency.

## Запрос для гравюр

Create a single transparent PNG asset atlas of EIGHT antique copperplate engraving marginal illustrations, arranged in a precise 4 column x 2 row layout of equally sized square cells with generous transparent padding between cells. Absolutely no visible grid, frames, labels, paper background or text. Each cell contains a distinct finely shaded naturalistic antique book engraving vignette with scanned ink grain, warm dark sepia crosshatching and stippling, tapered organic strokes, worn print imperfections, NOT vector icons or flat outlines. Row1 left to right: (1) intricate irregular spiderweb corner with small realistic spider and fine silk strands, (2) coiled octopus tentacle with detailed suckers and little ocean ripples, (3) dried antique blood droplets and scratched claw marks rendered as finely textured dark reddish brown engraving, (4) branching frost crystals and cracked ice with detailed etched hatching. Row2 left to right: (5) singed paper-like dark burned ragged edge flecks and smoke rendered in etched ink without any solid paper background, (6) strange complex occult astronomical seal with fine aged concentric hatchwork and tiny illegible characters, (7) three naturalistic monster claw gouges with textured fibrous edges and small ink flecks, (8) spilled ink with organic feathered edges, quill pen scratches and individual tiny satellite drops. Each vignette self contained in its own cell, floating on real transparency. The imagery must look like cropped illustrations scanned from an 1890 occult natural history book, realistically hand engraved, never a clean computer graphic. No modern symbols, no text captions. Consistent density and scale across all eight cells.
